Around this time of the year, I would have been grinding out math competitions and doing problems every day. The situation is quite different from that time, and I am just wandering, unable to find what I really want to do and happening to overthink everything. 

Really, many things has changed from when I did math competitions. Last blog post I mentioned how I was trying to trick myself into thinking that I was an amazing person, and it wasn't like that when I started doing math competitions. 

Everything slowly started falling apart when I didn't qualify for AIME. I started questioning myself, asking myself if I really tried hard, if there was any problems with the study plans, or if there was anything wrong with the mindset I had. I even tried doing other competitions like USAMTS or F=ma, believing that I can suceed if I manage to fix all the mistakes I have made. Yet nothing worked out and I was always barely off from the cutoff. It's not only that. I failed in other things I have previously suceeded in as well. Failing AP tests, not getting leadership roles, not being able to jump in any meets, not getting shortlisted in an essay competition, robotics skills breaking at competition, losing scioly medal due to miscommunication with the volunteer, losing prize at CMIMC due to one team making a mistake in their submission, my first hackathon that I did with my team failing with my ideas.

I remember last post I made on AoPS I was listing all of these and closing with: Oh yeah although I have failed, I have learned important lessons and I have grown a lot. 

I was always trying to look for positive things in my life. That was the way I was taught. That's the way Randy Paunch (One of my Role Models) taught. So I looked in that direction and it was actually quite comforting for myself.

I was quite ambitious when I first started math competitions. My goal was to prove to everyone that I am capable of doing this and show who I actually am. I thought that I could make USAMO if I tried hard, because I believed that I was talented and smart and all that. And from that perspective, my attitude has totally shifted. Nowadays, which is more than a month after the AoPS blog, I just think that I am above average in studies but not exceptionally talented, and more importantly, I think I simply got unlucky. 

I know I wasn't simply _unlucky_, but that's what my mind wants to think, so let me explain why I think that might have happened:

I have been getting rejected in every field I have attempted to do. Here, I don't think it's even necessary to question if I tried hard or not, or anything really to claim that attempting everything and failing  in everything will hurt. And for me, every time things didn't work out, a small wedge was driven into me. Although it was small, I did feel pain, and at the end of the day, it just put me in a place where I am on the edge of shattering. 

Then on, when I tried to think positive about myself and try to fake myself into thinking that I was an amazing person, and just laugh off saying that I got unlucky. That was the only way that I came to. I couldn't recover anything from reassessing my mistakes, and just looking for a success that would take out all the wedges didn't work, because every opportunity turned into an another wedge. Here, the only way I can continue to try to love myself and have a decent self esteem was to trick myself and save myself from shattering, completely losing hope, and continue on pursuing other things to meet the expectations of myself. 

I can just learn from my mistakes and continue on if it happens a couple of times. Thinking, "maybe next time I will succeed". But if that repeats over and over, I just start losing hope,  because I realize that the success may never come to me. Here, I just have to admit, that I was a insanely stupid person, or that I just didn't try my best. Both of those hurts me a lot. If I am a stupid person after all, that would just  prove that I cannot do anything to meet my expectations. If I didn't try my best and still have room to improve, I cannot see how I can improve if every attempt I make just fails. I really tried hard, and that is what I wanted to prove, that I can try hard and achieve what I wanted to do. I had a talk about why I was failing everything with someone close to me, and one thing they brought up was that I didn't seem to work hard preparing everything. Which got me mad, as I know that I cannot have tried harder than that, and also because that statement was just refusing what I wanted to prove the most.

There was an another option, which was to refuse everything that has happened, and classifying as unlucky. It was the most direct method to keep everything from collapsing. Thinking that everything that has happened was an accident and it says nothing about my personal qualities. I know it wasn't true, but that was the cleanest way to cover it up. I used up all my energy thinking about what I could have done better and how I can improve next time, complicating things. I had no answers that I can put up. Everything has been tested and it failed, and if I just manage to trick myself into thinking that it was all bad luck, then I didn't have to go through this endless witch hunt. 

It was fine. I didn't think about what I did anymore. My confidence was up, almost highest as it has ever been, and I was closer to becoming someone who I can love. The cracks has gotten reinforced with tape, and I was stronger again, capable of taking more wedges if possible. Thinking back again, it wasn't the best method, but it did the job and it seemed fine.

However, few days ago, when I finished reading The Fall by Camus, I was exposed again, and I learned that I was actually faking myself into thinking that it was just all unfortunate things and I was an amazing individual. The tape that reinforced the cracks were now peeled off, and the cracks seems a bigger than it was.

That is the current state that I am in. I don't want to just continue living with cracks reinforced with tape, so I am back in the position where I have to do something with the wedges and cracks, without any obvious answers that I can see. I need a method that can take all the wedges out (erase every failures) and stitch up the cracks. But if I leave it like this or take an another wedge, I will shatter and end up without any hope. 

And even this moment feels hopeless. I have been thinking about this since yesterday, and what mindset I should adapt or just how I should just go about this has been a challenging task for me to solve. What makes it harder is that I have experienced depression before and I just never want to go back to living without any hope or goals. 

I know life is supposed to have ups and downs, but every time I escape from downs, I just end up falling there again. I feel like I have improved as a person and I have shifted into better managing my emotions and preventing myself from falling, but it's just not avoidable. Still, I have hope that I may recover from this and have a period where I won't have to worry too much and be happy many times. So I will be trying my best to keep that hope.
